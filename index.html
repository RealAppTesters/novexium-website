The shift you cannot ignore

Search is moving to AI. Most websites are invisible.

40% of searches now happen inside AI engines. Your competitors are showing up in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity — and you might not be.

Search is changing fast

Traditional Google rankings still matter — but LLMs like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity now answer questions directly. If your website is not structured to be cited, crawled, and understood by AI engines, you are missing a growing share of traffic that your competitors are quietly capturing.

GEO: Generative Engine Optimisation

GEO is the emerging discipline of making your brand citable, trustworthy, and accessible to AI engines — not just Google. I review your site for LLM accessibility, identity signals, structured data, and citability factors that determine whether AI models surface your brand in their answers.

Why manual beats automated

Automated tools flag technical errors — but they cannot judge whether your homepage communicates trust, whether your messaging is confusing, or whether your content is something an LLM would actually cite. That human judgement is what separates a useful audit from a list of scores.

What I audit

Every angle that affects your growth

A manual, expert-led review across every dimension that impacts how your website is found, trusted, and converted.

SEO & technical health

Meta tags, heading structure, crawlability, internal linking, page speed, SSL, schema markup, sitemap integrity, image optimisation, and canonical tags. Every technical factor that search engines use to rank — and penalise — your site.

UX & conversion review

How visitors move through your site, where they get confused, where they drop off, and what stops them from taking action. Navigation clarity, CTA placement, visual hierarchy, mobile usability, and page-level messaging — reviewed with genuinely fresh eyes.

AI & GEO visibility

Whether your site is structured for LLM discoverability — including identity signals, authoritative content formatting, schema, and citability. As AI search continues to grow, this is the audit layer most businesses have never had done.

Trust & credibility signals

First impressions, branding consistency, social proof placement, contact accessibility, security indicators, and whether your site communicates authority before a visitor reads a single sentence. Weak trust costs you conversions silently.

Landing page analysis

Your landing page has one job: convert visitors into leads or customers. I review headline clarity, above-the-fold messaging, value proposition strength, CTA placement, objection handling, and social proof — and tell you precisely what is undermining conversions.

Competitor comparison

How does your site measure up against top performers in your space? I compare your SEO signals, content depth, trust indicators, and conversion structure against key competitors — so you can see exactly where you are being outranked and why.
